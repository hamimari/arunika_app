## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Navigate to Koleksi with pre-applied category filter
When the user taps an AR card category on the home screen, the app SHALL open Kartu AR inside the Belajar tab with the tapped category's ID, and the collection screen SHALL apply that filter on load.

#### Scenario: Category tap on home opens filtered Koleksi
- **WHEN** the user taps an AR category chip/card on the home screen
- **THEN** the Belajar tab opens on Kartu AR and the grid is pre-filtered to show only cards in that category
