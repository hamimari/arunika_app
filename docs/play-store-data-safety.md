# Play Console Data Safety — Source of Truth

This document maps every data type Arunika actually collects (as of the `add-play-store-release-compliance`
change) to Google Play Console's Data Safety form categories. Use this as the source of truth when filling out
the form in Play Console — don't guess from memory, and update this file whenever data collection changes.

Play Console path: **App content → Data safety**.

## Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?

**Yes.**

## Data NOT collected

- **Precise or approximate location** — not collected.
- **Health and fitness data** — not collected (growth/weight-height tracking, if present, is entered manually by
  the parent and stored only as part of the account; treat as "Personal info" if re-assessed, not health data).
- **Photos or videos** — not collected from the device; the app does not access the camera roll (AR camera
  access is used live for scanning, not stored).
- **Contacts** — not collected.
- **Advertising ID** — not collected; Arunika has no ads SDK.
- **Web browsing history, search history (outside app), messages** — not collected.

## Security practices

- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and Arunika's backend, and between the backend and
  every third-party processor (Midtrans, Google Play, Firebase).
- Users can request account and data deletion — see the `account-deletion` capability: in-app ("Hapus Akun" in
  Profile) or via the public web page (`account-deletion.html` on the marketing site), independent of login.
- Passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt), never in plaintext.

## Target audience

Arunika is directed, in part, at children (a parent creates a child profile within their own account). When
filling out the **Target audience and content** section in Play Console:
- Select the appropriate child age range(s) in addition to any adult range, since the app is used by children
  under adult supervision.
- Confirm "Ads" = **No** (the app has no ads SDK).
- Confirm the app does not include any general-audience content inappropriate for the selected child age range.
